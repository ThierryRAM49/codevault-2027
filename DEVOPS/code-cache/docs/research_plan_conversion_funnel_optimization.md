# Conversion Funnel Optimization Research Plan - 2025

## Task Overview
Research comprehensive conversion funnel optimization techniques and psychology for 2025 web applications, covering 10 specific focus areas and producing a detailed analysis document.

## Research Areas (10 Focus Points)
1. **Modern conversion funnel design patterns** - Current best practices and emerging patterns
2. **User journey mapping and optimization points** - Mapping techniques and key optimization opportunities
3. **Friction reduction strategies throughout the funnel** - Identifying and eliminating conversion barriers
4. **Progressive disclosure and staged commitment** - Gradual information revelation and commitment escalation
5. **Social proof and urgency implementation** - Psychological triggers for conversion enhancement
6. **Mobile conversion optimization** - Mobile-specific optimization strategies
7. **Behavioral psychology in conversion design** - Psychological principles driving conversions
8. **Analytics and conversion tracking best practices** - Measurement and optimization methodologies
9. **Exit-intent and retention strategies** - Preventing abandonment and re-engaging users
10. **Multi-step vs single-step conversion approaches** - Comparative analysis of funnel structures

## Research Strategy
- **Task Type**: Verification-focused with comprehensive analysis
- **Sources**: Mix of industry reports, UX/CRO expertise sites, behavioral psychology research, and current best practices
- **Validation**: Cross-reference findings across multiple authoritative sources
- **Documentation**: Track all sources using sources_add tool
